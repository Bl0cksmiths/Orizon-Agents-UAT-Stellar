## 3 · RC-01 — the numbers before and after

`GET /api/stellar/reputation/agt_09l5` on the drill backend, before either
dispute was opened and after both were upheld:

| Run | | `count` | `disputed` | `dispute_rate_bps` | `smoothed_bps` | `avg_bps` | `weight` (stroops) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| TTL 15 s | before | 2 | 0 | 0 | 7032 | 9000 | 2 000 000 |
| | after | **4** | **2** | **5000** | **6935** | 5000 | 4 000 000 |
| TTL 120 s | before | 6 | 2 | 3333 | 6968 | 6333 | 6 000 000 |
| | after | **8** | **4** | **5000** | **6875** | 5000 | 8 000 000 |

For each uphold, `count` rose by one and so did `disputed`: two upheld
disputes added two each. `dispute_rate_bps` rose and the smoothed score fell.
In the 120 s run the uphold script printed its own before and after for its one dispute:
"before this run: 3333 bps (2 of 6), after this run: 4285 bps (3 of 7), moved
+952 bps".

The settler's own rating was not withdrawn. The average moved to the weighted
mean of all ratings: (9000 × 2 + 1000 × 2) / 4 = 5000. It did not become the
dispute's 10/100. With the settler at 90 and the dispute at 10, each pair
averages 50, inside the 15–52.5 the story expects.

## 5 · RC-04 — the next plan

Right after each uphold, the drill decomposed "Build me a calculator app"; the
kit's research step routes to agt_09l5. While the script ran, a poller kept the
server's read cache warm by reading the reputation route every 0.5 s, as a busy
console does.

| Run · path | Plan decomposed | Plan's stamp for agt_09l5 | Route before the uphold | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 15 s · API | at once | `rep_count` 4, `rep_dispute_rate_bps` 5000, `rep_bps` 6935 | 3, 3333 | **Pass**: the new score |
| 120 s · API | at once | `rep_count` 8, `rep_dispute_rate_bps` 5000, `rep_bps` 6875 | 7, 4285 | **Pass**: the new score |
| 15 s · script | 5.8 s after the rating landed | `rep_count` 3, `rep_dispute_rate_bps` 3333 | 2, 0 | new, but only because the cache had expired: the server served the old count for 2.2 s |
| 120 s · script | 4.3 s after the rating landed | `rep_count` 6, `rep_dispute_rate_bps` 3333, `rep_bps` 6968 | 6, 3333 | **Fail**: the pre-dispute score, served for 91.9 s (D-066) |

The adjudication route invalidates the agent's cached score in the process that
serves plans, so the next plan is right at once. The uphold script invalidates
only its own process's cache, so a running server keeps the old score until its
read TTL runs out. At 15 s that stale window can fall inside the story's "within
a few seconds", or, as in the 15 s run, happen to miss it. The 120 s run widens
the window so the gap cannot hide. Logged as **D-066** (Major).

## 6 · RC-05 — an open dispute moves nothing

Both disputes were opened (signed challenge, `status` `open`) and left
unadjudicated. The reputation was then read at once, and read again after the
read TTL, when it came from the ledger rather than the cache. A plan was
decomposed as well:

| Run | Route at once | Route after the TTL | Plan stamp |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 15 s | 2 / 0 / 0 / 7032 | 2 / 0 / 0 / 7032 | 2, 0 bps, 7032 |
| 120 s | 6 / 2 / 3333 / 6968 | 6 / 2 / 3333 / 6968 | 6, 3333 bps, 6968 |

(`count` / `disputed` / `dispute_rate_bps` / `smoothed_bps`, the same as
before the disputes were opened.) Opening a dispute writes nothing to the
ledger, so disputing costs the agent nothing until it is upheld.
