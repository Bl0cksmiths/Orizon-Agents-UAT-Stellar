## 3 · The story's baseline no longer holds

The story was written against 11 outside agents from 7 outside wallets, one of
them bound. On 2026-10-03 `GET /api/agents` lists 541 agents: 12 built-in
catalog agents, 13 owned by team wallets, and 516 on-chain agents whose owner
is not in the team register, from 511 owners. Three of the 516 are bound.

A random sample of 25 of those 511 owners, read on Horizon testnet: every one
was created by friendbot, 24 of them between 2026-10-01 and 2026-10-03 and one
on 2026-05-31. Each owns one agent with a distinct name.

This report draws no conclusion about who runs them. The adoption report counts
any owner outside the team register as an outside operator (SOW §6.3), so the
two agent and wallet targets read met; whether these registrations are the
outside adoption §6.3 means is a question for the product owner, not a finding
this run can settle. Their number is what makes the report slow (D-091).

The report's 514 agents and 509 wallets are two fewer of each than the 516
and 511 the marketplace lists for the same register, read minutes apart. With
`degraded: true` the report's totals are a floor by its own contract, so this
is recorded, not filed.
