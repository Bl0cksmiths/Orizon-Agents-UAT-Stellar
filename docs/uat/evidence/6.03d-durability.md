**DU-05 on the deploy.** The startup log cannot be read from outside, and no
Render log access was available. Both stores are chosen from one variable,
`DATABASE_URL`, so the surviving binding is the deploy's own proof that it is
set: the dispute store is Postgres too. `tests/durability.spec.ts` asserts this
on every suite run. It fails if the binding is ever newer than the process, or
gone.
