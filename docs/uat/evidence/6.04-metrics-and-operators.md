## 1 · How it counts

The verifier is `tools/sow-metrics-verify/`. It does not call the backend's
counting code or its SDK. It reads the chain with raw JSON-RPC and Horizon
calls and decodes the XDR itself (`xdr.ts`, `scval.ts`, `strkey.ts`):

- **Agents and owners**: `AgentRegistry.list_ids()` and `get(id)` by RPC
  simulation.
- **Charges**: every id an escrow's instance `Nonce` has issued
  (`0..Nonce-1`), each read as `receipt(id)` or `authorization(id)`, for the
  live escrow v2 and the old v1. This is the escrow's complete history,
  whatever the RPC's event retention. Each charge in the event window is
  cross-checked against the escrow's own `charged` event.
- **Disputes**: every `ReputationLedger.submit` in the platform keys' Horizon
  histories, cross-checked against the ledger's lifetime
  `rep_state(id).disputed` for every agent.
- **Refunds**: every native-asset-contract `transfer` signed by a platform key
  since the sprint began (2026-09-07), with its amount checked against the
  balance change Horizon recorded.
- **Who is the team**: the committed register (`app/data/team_wallets.json` on
  the backend's main branch, 10 wallets) plus every key the platform runs, read
  live: the API's network document, `/readiness`, the registry's `admin()`, and
  the admin, settler, scorer and sealer each contract stores. All three platform
  keys (GA7AI5…5OQV, GB5MKH…KCMR, GDB4N25…CDHP) are in the register.
- **Milestones** (m06 to m11): the live site and API with no session and no
  redirects followed, and GitHub's unauthenticated API.

The backend's own method (`docs/operators/sow-metrics.md`) was used only as a
reference for the rules (what is a self-payment, what is a workflow). The
backend generator was not run.

Each count is taken at the moment the index says that row was measured. Its
method names the times: m04 at 10:42 UTC, m05 and m08 at 15:30 UTC, m01 and m02
at 15:48 UTC, all on 2026-09-30. The end-of-day and today's counts are recorded
next to them.
