# 6.03a evidence — the dispute happy path, end to end on testnet

Attempted 2026-09-24 against `https://orizons.xyz` (backend
`orizon-agents-be-stellar.onrender.com`). Testnet only.

> **The story could not be executed.** Its flow starts from a settled payment,
> and no payment settles on this deployment. Two of the three backend
> preconditions fail. Nothing below is a rehearsal or a simulation: it is what
> the deployed service did when the buyer side was driven for real.

## 3 · Where the story stops

Step 2 is *"On the trace page, confirm the receipt panel appears above the
tabs: window countdown, each settled step, its charge."* There is no receipt to
appear:

```
GET /api/tasks/tsk_7fc5bc5ea95f15fc/disputes
{"task_id":"tsk_7fc5bc5ea95f15fc","window_closes_at":null,"now":1790227970.2,
 "settlement":null,"disputes":[]}
```

Raising one by hand against the run's own job id is refused, because the job was
never sealed:

```
POST /api/disputes/challenge {"job_id_hex":"7fc5bc5ea95f15fc…","step_index":0}
404 {"detail":"unknown_job","error":{"code":"unknown_job","request_id":"5a0cad0e2b424a6d"}}
```

So the flow cannot reach step 3 (raise a dispute), and therefore not step 4
(Dan's `--dry-run` preview), step 5 (the live uphold), step 6 (the receipt
flipping to "Refunded"), or step 7 (both transactions on Stellar Expert).

**Two independent blockers, either of which alone stops the story:**

1. **No settlement is ever recorded** — D-050 (backend#67), behind D-039
   (contracts#3): `PaymentEscrow.charge` cannot move the payer's funds, so
   `_record_settlement` returns early and no dispute window opens.
2. **Refunds are switched off in the deployment** — D-051: the uphold route
   answers `503 dispute_refunds_disabled` before it even checks the adjudicator
   key, so even a settled dispute could not be credited today.
