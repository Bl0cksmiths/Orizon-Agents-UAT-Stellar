# Story 6.04 — independent verification of every on-chain claim

Story 6.04 is the last gate before the completion report goes to the Ambassador
Chapter Lead. Its question is narrow: can every claim the sprint makes be checked
by someone who does not trust the team? A transaction that resolves but shows
something other than its claim counts as a failure, and a worse one than a dead link.
