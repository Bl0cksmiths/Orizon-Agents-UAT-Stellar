# 6.10 evidence — RV-01 and RV-02, every link on the evidence index read for honesty

Reviewed 2026-10-03 against the index frontend `main` deploys
(`049f3d52`, `content/evidence/index.json`), which
`https://orizons.xyz/evidence` renders: 218 links, 141 distinct URLs, 59
distinct transactions. The page heads itself "As of September 30, 2026".
Criteria: RV-01 and RV-02 in `docs/uat/test-plan.md`.

## Method

- **Transactions (59).** Each was read from Horizon testnet
  (`/transactions/<hash>/operations`) with `tools/onchain-verify/horizon.ts`
  and compared with `tools/onchain-verify/facts.ts`: all 48 team transactions
  match their pinned signer, contract, function, arguments, transfers and UTC
  day. Each signer is named below by its role in the team register
  (`app/data/team_wallets.json`). Each of the 11 outside transactions was
  checked by rule: one `register` call on the AgentRegistry, signed by a wallet
  outside the register that names itself owner, on the date the label gives,
  for the agent id the label gives.
- **Contracts and accounts.** Counts were read today: the registry through
  Stellar RPC (`readAgents`), and ratings, seals and settles from the full
  Horizon history of the admin wallet and the platform key.
- **Web pages.** Every page, API route, pull request, repository, file and
  video was opened over HTTP without logging in. Pull request merge times come
  from each page's `mergedTime`, video lengths and dates from each YouTube
  page, licences from each repository page. The screenshots and the D3-c
  recording were downloaded and looked at, the recording at four points of its 13.7 s. The
  live receipt (`6.1-D3-c #5`) was rendered in Chromium. The live plan behind
  D2 was read through `POST /api/orchestrator/decompose`.
- **Inputs.** The frontend's own verifier run of today (216 pass, 2
  unverified) shows that the links resolve. This review asks whether each one
  is honest, which the verifier does not check.

## Consent

Eleven transactions and seven wallets on the index belong to outside
operators: under `6.1-D1-c`, `6.1-D4-c`, `m01`, `m02` and `m06`. Their
agent names, wallets and hashes are not repeated here. Their rows read
"outside registration (withheld, D-092)" or "outside operator wallet
(withheld, D-092)" and are judged by the rule above. Team wallets and team
agents are named.

## Verdicts

- **true**: what the link shows matches the label: who signed (by team
  role), what was done, the UTC date and the amount.
- **misleading**: the link is real, but a reader comes away believing
  something the link does not show.
- **false**: the label states something the link contradicts.
- **does not resolve**: no answer, or nothing a reader can use.

Each position is `<item> #<n>`, the n-th link of the item in index order.
