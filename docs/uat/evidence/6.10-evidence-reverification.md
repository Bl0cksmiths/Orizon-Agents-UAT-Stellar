## 3 · Outside operators on the page (RV-04)

The story's premise is that outside operators' ids, wallets and hashes are
deliberately absent and the index links `/app/ecosystem` instead. They are
not absent. On 2026-10-03 the page shows 11 outside agents' names, 7 outside
wallets (abbreviated in labels, in full in 7 account links) and the 11 hashes
of their registrations, in items `6.1-D1-c`, `6.1-D4-c`, `m01`, `m02` and `m06`.
None appears in the disclosures, notes or snapshot text. The snapshot's own
method note says the links were added on 2026-10-01 because "the platform
lists outside operators publicly". The backend's
`docs/operators/sow-metrics.md#consent` says none is published until consent
is recorded, and no consent is recorded. Filed as **D-092 (Critical)**.
