# 6.09 checklist — onboarding from the guide, readiness and a real phone

The parts of story 6.09 that automation cannot reach: Freighter signing, a
deployed reference agent, and a real phone with a screen reader. It covers
OB-01, OB-05, OB-06 and the screen-reader half of OB-09 (`docs/uat/test-plan.md`,
"Acceptance criteria — OB"). Everything else in 6.09 is checked by
`tests/operator-onboarding.spec.ts`.

Work through it in order and fill in every **Record** field as you go. Results
go into `docs/uat/evidence/6.09-operator-onboarding.md`; screenshots are named
`6.09-<step>.png` (for example `6.09-A3.png`). Copy expected text *exactly* as
quoted: a difference is a finding, even a small one.

## Rules

- **Consent.** Never write an outside operator's agent id, wallet or transaction
  hash anywhere: not in the evidence file, a screenshot name, a defect, a
  friction row or a chat message. Outside operators are `OP-1`, `OP-2`… and their
  agents are named by position only ("OP-1's first agent"). Crop or blur them
  out of screenshots. Your own QA agent id, the QA wallet and your own tx hashes
  are team identifiers: record them in full in the evidence file.
- **A team wallet only.** Register from a wallet already declared in the backend's
  team register (`app/data/team_wallets.json`), normally the "QA throwaway
  operator key" `GBWMD26IB6CMG3JO3HU7SD7ZJSTF4BIJ5JS77ANMLJ52M6FV6K3J7BQJ`. An
  agent owned by an undeclared wallet is counted as an outside operator and
  inflates the SOW §6.3 adoption figures. If you must use a fresh wallet, it is
  added to the register (backend pull request, merged and **deployed**) before
  the agent is registered, and the deploy is confirmed by the new address
  appearing under `excluded` in `GET /api/ecosystem/adoption`.
- **Testnet only.** Freighter on **Test Net** before you open orizons.xyz.
- **One fresh agent id.** Use `uat609_qa_1` (`_2` if it is taken or a run
  fails part way). Never reuse an id from an earlier story.
- **The guide is the only instruction.** Follow `/guide/list-your-agent`
  v1.1.0 literally. Where the product and the guide disagree, do what the
  product needs, and log the mismatch (see [Mismatch log](#mismatch-log)).
- **Never bind someone else's domain.** Bind only the reference agent you deploy
  yourself (F-035). The parked-page case (OB-03) is observed on OP-1's agent by
  the spec, never re-created by QA.
- **Do not clean up.** Leave the registration and the final binding in place;
  they are evidence.
